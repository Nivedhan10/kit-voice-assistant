1.
About
KIT
Knotopian Institute of Technology (KIT) is an autonomous engineering institution in Coimbatore,
Tamil Nadu, known for high academic standards and strong industry placements. The campus,
Knotopian Knowledge City, has modern laboratories, a central library with a digital collection,
separate hostels for men and women, and college buses on 22 routes across the city.
KIT offers four-year B.Tech programmes in three disciplines and a two-year M.Tech programme. This
brochure covers B.Tech admissions only.

Other
conditions
Applicants must have passed the Higher Secondary examination (12th standard) or an equivalent,
with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics.
Applicants appearing for the 12th examination in 2027 may apply; admission is confirmed only
after the results are published and the minimum is met.
Applicants must be at least 17 years old on 31 December 2027.

3.
How
to
apply
1. Apply online through the KIT admissions portal or the admissions assistant on the KIT website.
One application is needed for each programme you want to be considered for.
2. Give your full name, mobile number, email, the programme (course code) and your 12th
percentage, or expected percentage if your results are awaited.
3. You will receive an application number by email. There is no application fee.
4. The merit list is published on the website on the date in section 4. Selected applicants receive an
admission letter by email.
5. Report for document verification and fee payment on the counselling dates in section 4.

7.
Life
at
KIT
Hostels
Separate hostels for men and women, with rooms shared by two or three students. Rooms are allotted
by department block. Wi-Fi, a reading room and a gym are available in every block.
Transport
College buses run on 22 routes across Coimbatore. Bus fares depend on the distance band of the route
and are paid per semester.
Placements
In 2025–26, 412 job offers were made to the final-year B.Tech students of KIT. The highest offer was
₹24 lakh per annum and the median offer was ₹5.8 lakh per annum. Leading recruiters included
Zoho, TCS, Freshworks and several product start-ups. Placement drives are open only to final-year
students.
8.
Frequently
asked
questions
Can I apply for more than one programme? Yes. Submit one application for each programme.
Each is considered on merit.

My 12th results are not out yet. Can I apply? Yes. Give your expected percentage and update
your actual marks by 15 May 2027.
Is there an entrance examination? No. Admission is on 12th marks only.
Can I change my programme after admission? A change of programme in the first year is
allowed only if a seat is vacant and your 12th percentage meets the minimum of the new programme.
Is the hostel compulsory? No. Day scholars can use the college buses.
