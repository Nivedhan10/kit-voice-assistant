Circular 12/2026
Date: 15 October 2026

Subject:
Change
to
the
time
allowed
for
submitting
on-duty
proof
(Rule
6.4.3)
Many on-duty requests are being decided weeks after the event because proof arrives late. The
Academic Council has therefore approved the following change to the Student Handbook 2026.
1. With effect from 1 November 2026, a B.Tech student must submit proof of participation for an on-
duty request (a participation certificate or a letter from the organiser) within 3 working days after
the event, instead of the 5 working days in Rule 6.4.3 of the Student Handbook 2026.
2. On-duty requests for events held before 1 November 2026 follow the earlier 5-working-day rule.
3. If proof is not submitted within 3 working days, the on-duty request is rejected and the periods are
counted as absent, as stated in Rule 6.4.4.
4. All other rules on on-duty in Chapter 6 remain unchanged.
Dr. V. Anitha
Dean (Academics)
